The toolbar on the canvas picks the tool. **Layers** and **Story** on the right edit the shown page's layers and text, and a bar beside selected text sets its type.

## Navigate and select

Use **Pan** to move the view and the zoom slider in the status bar to change zoom. **View → Fit Window**, `Ctrl+0` or `Command+0`, fits the active page. Switching pages fits the new page.

Use **Select** to pick layers. Click a layer, hold `Shift`, `Ctrl`, or `Command` to add or remove one, or drag from an empty spot to select with a marquee. Drag a selection to move it. Resize and rotate handles appear for a single selection; a multiple selection can only be moved. Locked layers do not move.

## Choose an editing tool

<Tabs>
  <Tab title='Direct Selection'>
    Select a text block or text layer with **Direct Selection** to show the anchors of its frame.
    Drag an anchor or a segment to reshape the frame, `Shift`-click to pick more anchors, or drag
    from an empty spot to pick the anchors inside a marquee. `Delete` or `Backspace` removes the
    picked anchors, keeping at least three. The text fits the new shape when the drag ends.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title='Text'>
    Click with **Text** to create point text, or drag to create a text box. Type the text in place,
    then adjust [typography](/guides/typesetting). With a text block or text layer selected, the
    **Text** tool edits its translation in place.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title='Brush and Eraser'>
    **Brush** paints with the chosen color and size on the selected raster layer, or creates a new
    raster layer at the top of the page when none is selected. **Eraser** needs a selected raster
    layer. Both also paint a text block's clean-up or mask selected in **Layers**; on a mask,
    **Brush** shows more of the clean-up and **Eraser** hides it. Both skip hidden or locked layers.
    Hold `Alt` and scroll to change the size.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title='Color picker'>
    Click with **Color picker** to sample the page's own pixel under the pointer. The sampled color
    becomes the brush color, or, when you started from a color field's **Pick color from canvas**,
    that field's color.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title='Remove'>
    Paint over source marks with **Remove**. Releasing the stroke inpaints that area right away and
    adds the result as a new raster layer limited to the painted area. See
    [cleanup](/guides/cleanup).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Hold `Space` or the middle mouse button and drag to pan without changing tools. Scroll to pan,
  hold `Shift` to pan horizontally, or hold `Ctrl` or `Command` to zoom at the pointer; a trackpad
  pinch also zooms. `Escape` clears the selection.
</Tip>

Single-key tool shortcuts are inactive while a text field is focused. See [keyboard shortcuts](/reference/shortcuts).
