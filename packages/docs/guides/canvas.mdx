The toolbar on the canvas picks the tool. **Layers** on the right edits the shown page's layers and **Story** every page's text, and a bar beside selected text sets its type.

## Navigate and select

Use **Pan** to move the view and the zoom slider in the status bar to change zoom. **View → Fit Window**, `Ctrl+0` or `Command+0`, fits the active page. Switching pages fits the new page.

Use **Select** to pick layers. Click a layer, hold `Shift`, `Ctrl`, or `Command` to add or remove one, or drag from an empty spot to select with a marquee. Drag a selection to move it. Resize and rotate handles appear for a single selection; a multiple selection can only be moved. Locked layers do not move.

## Choose an editing tool

<Tabs>
  <Tab title='Direct Selection'>
    Select a text block or text layer with **Direct Selection** to show the anchors of its frame.
    Drag an anchor or a segment to reshape the frame, `Shift`-click to pick more anchors, or drag
    from an empty spot to pick the anchors inside a marquee. `Delete` or `Backspace` removes the
    picked anchors, keeping at least three. The text fits the new shape when the drag ends.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title='Marquee and Lasso'>
    Drag with **Rectangular Marquee** to mark a rectangle of the page, or with **Lasso** to mark a
    freehand shape, as in Photoshop. Hold `Shift` as you start a drag to add to the marked area, or
    `Alt` to take from it. A click without a drag, or `Ctrl+D` or `Command+D`, deselects it. The bar
    below the area is **Generative Fill**. Pick the model it draws with, FLUX.3 Klein unless you
    pick another: it's the fill's own, apart from the inpainting model in **Settings → Pipeline**,
    which cleans pages and **Remove**'s strokes. Describe what to draw there and choose
    **Generate**, or leave the prompt empty to use the **Prompt** in **Settings → Pipeline**. The
    result is added as a new raster layer limited to the area, named after your prompt. The area
    stays marked, so you can generate again, and a fill model that Settings doesn't use stays loaded
    for five minutes after the last run, so generating again doesn't load it again. LaMa takes no
    prompt, so with it the bar has no prompt field.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title='Text'>
    Click with **Text** to start point text, or drag to start a text box, and type on the canvas, as
    in Photoshop. Click a text with **Text**, or double-click it with **Select**, to type into it
    where you clicked; drag across it to select characters, or double-click a word. `Ctrl`+`Enter`
    or `Enter` on the number pad keeps what you typed, as clicking away or choosing another tool
    does, and `Esc` drops it. Each time you type into a text is one undo step, and a new text is
    added only once it has characters. Then adjust [typography](/guides/typesetting).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title='Text block'>
    Drag with **Text block** over text that detection missed. It adds a text block with the box as
    its frame and its mask, in the balloon or panel under it. Then read it with **OCR** from its
    menu in **Story**. Paint its mask smaller to keep the art around the text.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title='Brush and Eraser'>
    **Brush** paints with the chosen color and size on the selected raster layer, or creates a new
    raster layer at the top of the page when none is selected. **Eraser** needs a selected raster
    layer. Both also paint a text block's clean-up or mask selected in **Layers**; on a mask,
    **Brush** shows more of the clean-up and **Eraser** hides it. Both skip hidden or locked layers.
    Hold `Alt` and scroll to change the size.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title='Color picker'>
    Click with **Color picker** to sample the page's own pixel under the pointer. The sampled color
    becomes the brush color, or, when you started from a color field's **Pick color from canvas**,
    that field's color.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title='Remove'>
    Paint over source marks with **Remove**. Releasing the stroke inpaints that area right away. The
    result goes onto the raster layer, or the text block's clean-up, selected in **Layers**, or onto
    a new raster layer that stays selected for the next strokes. See [cleanup](/guides/cleanup).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Hold `Space` or the middle mouse button and drag to pan without changing tools. Scroll to pan,
  hold `Shift` to pan horizontally, or hold `Ctrl` or `Command` to zoom at the pointer; a trackpad
  pinch also zooms. `Escape` clears the selection.
</Tip>

Single-key tool shortcuts are inactive while a text field is focused. See [keyboard shortcuts](/reference/shortcuts).
