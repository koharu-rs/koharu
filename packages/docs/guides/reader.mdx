Switch between **Read** and **Edit** in the title bar. **Read** shows the project's pages alone, fit to the window, without the page list or the panels; nothing can be edited there. **Edit** returns to the editor on the page you were reading.

## Turn pages

Click the left or right half of the page, use the arrow keys, or scroll. Manga turns right to left, so the left half and `←` go forward and the right half and `→` go back; a left-to-right book is the other way round. `↓`, `Page Down`, and `Space` always go forward, `↑` and `Page Up` back, and `Home` and `End` go to the first and last page. See [keyboard shortcuts](/reference/shortcuts#reader).

A chapter from the [gallery](/guides/gallery) turns the way its site says, and a webtoon's strip turns forward downward. For any other project, choose **Right to left** or **Left to right** in the page indicator; Koharu remembers the choice.

## Two-page spreads

While the window is wider than tall, the reader shows two pages side by side, as an open book does: for a right-to-left book, the later page is on the left. A webtoon's strip and a narrow window show one page at a time.

The first page stands alone, as a book's cover does, unless the chapter's site places it otherwise. When the pages pair up wrongly, for example when a two-page illustration is split across spreads, toggle **Cover alone** in the page indicator to shift the pairing by one page. Turning moves a whole spread.

## The page indicator

The indicator stays out of the page's way until the pointer comes near the bottom of the window. It shows the page or spread you're on, such as **4–5 / 21**, with **Cover alone** while spreads show, and the direction choice for a project that isn't from the gallery.
