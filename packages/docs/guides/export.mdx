---
title: 'Export PNG, PSD, CBZ, and PDF'
description: 'Export every page, or the selected ones, as flattened images, layered documents, a comic archive, or a PDF.'
---

Review the pages before exporting. **File → Export Project** writes every page of the project, and **File → Export Selected Pages** writes only the pages selected in the page list with `Ctrl`, `Command`, or `Shift`. Both write them in project order.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a format">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="PNG">
        One flattened image per page for delivery and review, drawn as the canvas draws it.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="PSD">
        One layered Photoshop document per page for further typography or artwork edits. Every layer stays editable: the scan is the bottom pixel layer, panels and balloons become folders, and each text block becomes a folder holding its clean-up as a pixel layer masked by the block's mask, its source as a hidden type layer, and its translation as a type layer. Visibility, opacity, blend modes, and locks are kept. Bold and italic that a font has no face for are written as Photoshop's Faux Bold and Faux Italic. Photoshop has no ruby, so ruby shows until you edit its type layer. Pages larger than 30,000 pixels on a side cannot be exported as PSD.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="CBZ">
        One comic archive of the flattened PNG pages, for readers that open CBZ files.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="PDF">
        One document with a flattened image per page, without loss, at 300 DPI. Its text is part of the image, so it cannot be selected or searched.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a destination">
    For PNG and PSD, select a folder in the native dialog. Files are numbered by the page's place in the project, with at least three digits:

    ```text
    001.png
    002.png
    ```

    A selected page keeps the number it has in the project, so exporting it again replaces its own file.

    For CBZ and PDF, choose where to save the file; it is named after the project by default. A CBZ's pages are numbered the same way. Existing files with the same names are overwritten.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  PSD is an interchange format. It does not keep model provenance, undo history, or Koharu's
  automatic fitting. Keep the `.khr` project file as your editable source and backup.
</Note>

## Check consistency

The canvas, PNG, CBZ, PDF, and PSD all draw through the same renderer. If they differ, report the page, format, and images through [troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting).

There is no separate “inpainted image only” export.
