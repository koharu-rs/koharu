The **Gallery** on the start screen reads manga sites built into Koharu. Open a chapter, and its pages become a project you can process, edit, and export like any other.

## Find a series

The search box searches every site at once and shows a row for each site that finds something; a site that finds nothing or doesn't answer is left out. Paste a series' or chapter's address instead to open it directly.

Without a search, the gallery shows **Your series**, the series you opened, most recent first, and each site's lists: **Popular**, **Latest**, or **Ongoing**, the series it is running. **See all** opens a list as a grid that loads more as you scroll. Koharu keeps each list for five minutes, so coming back doesn't ask the sites again.

## Open a series

Opening a series lists its chapters and adds it to **Your series**. A **Locked** chapter is paid or otherwise not readable with your account: sign in to the site, or buy it there. Remove a series from **Your series** with its **×** button; the projects of its chapters stay.

## Read a chapter into a project

<Steps>
  <Step title='Choose a chapter'>
    Its row shows a spinner while Koharu reads the chapter from the site.
  </Step>
  <Step title='Let its pages arrive'>
    The chapter opens as a project named after the series, the chapter, and the site, in **Edit**
    mode. Its pages download in order while the **Activity** panel shows **Loading pages**; you can
    work on the pages already there. The pages are stored in the project file, so the project
    doesn't need the site afterwards.
  </Step>
  <Step title='Process when you choose'>
    Nothing runs by itself. Choose **Run** when you are ready; see [processing](/guides/processing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Opening the same chapter again reopens its project and keeps the pages it already has. Koharu also remembers how the chapter reads, its direction and which side of a spread its first page takes, for the [reader](/guides/reader).

## Sign in to a site

Open **Settings → Gallery**. **Sign in** opens the site's own sign-in page in a window of its own; the GigaViewer sites, such as 少年ジャンプ＋, open their front page, where the header's login button signs in. Close the window when you're done, and Koharu asks the site who is signed in: a site that tells shows **Signed in as** and the account. **Sign out** clears that site's sign-in.

<Note>
  Koharu never sees your password: a site's sign-in lives in Koharu's built-in browser, which
  requests the site's pages as your browser would. Each site's terms apply to what you read and
  translate.
</Note>
