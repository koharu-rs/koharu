Inpainting reconstructs the artwork beneath source text. A precise removal area usually matters more than a larger model.

## Review automatic cleanup

Detection gives each text block a mask over its source glyphs, reaching a few pixels past them but never into another text's box. Inpainting fills each mask and stores the result as the block's clean-up, shown under its translation. Rerunning inpainting replaces the clean-up.

Most lettering sits on plain paper or a gradient, so Koharu fills it without the model when the surrounding pixels allow:

| Surroundings                                  | Fill                                    |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| One flat color                                | That color                              |
| A smooth background                           | Telea's fast-marching fill, from OpenCV |
| Line art, screentone, a bubble border, or art | The inpainting model                    |

The model redraws each text with 256 pixels of context around it, at the page's own scale, and never across two panels. LaMa's fill stays inside the mask. FLUX.3 Klein and Qwen-Image 2.1 Turbo redraw the whole window, and the clean-up keeps it around the text, shown through the block's mask, so painting the mask larger shows more of their redraw.

Inspect the result at normal reading size and high zoom. Look for glyph fragments, missed punctuation or furigana, erased bubble borders, repeated textures, and smeared screentone.

## Repair a missed region

<Steps>
  <Step title='Paint the area'>
    Choose **Remove**, set the brush size, and cover only the source marks that need reconstruction.
    Use controlled strokes and avoid neighboring line art.
  </Step>
  <Step title='Let inpainting run'>
    Release the stroke. Koharu inpaints that area at once with the selected inpainting model and
    adds the result as a new raster layer, limited to the painted area by its mask. It works from
    the page as it looks now, without the translated text, so another stroke over a result refines
    it.
  </Step>
  <Step title='Touch up and inspect'>
    Use **Color picker**, **Brush**, and **Eraser** for small deterministic corrections. Check the
    result again at normal zoom.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To start a text over, delete its text block: its clean-up goes with it and the original lettering shows again.

## Choose a model

Choose the inpainting model in **Settings → Pipeline**. **LaMa** is the default and takes no prompt. **FLUX.3 Klein** and **Qwen-Image 2.1 Turbo** are generative alternatives that follow the **Prompt** there and need more memory and time. See [vision and inpainting models](/models/vision).

<Warning>
  **Brush** strokes land on the selected raster layer, a Remove result included. Clear the selection
  before painting to keep manual touch-ups on a new raster layer.
</Warning>
